Process

Four phases. No black box.

You see the plan, the daily log, and the blockers. A typical mid-size deployment runs about four weeks.

  1. Week 0

    Scope

    A 45-minute call with your team and the customer. We leave with a written plan and a fixed definition of done.

  2. Week 1

    Embed

    Your FDE joins the customer's channels, gets access, and maps every system the deployment touches.

  3. Weeks 2–3

    Build

    Integrations, migration, and security work ship behind a pilot group with daily progress notes.

  4. Week 4

    Hand off

    Full rollout, runbooks, training, and a field report for your product team.

FAQ

Common questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask an engineer directly.

What does a Forward Deployed Engineer actually do?

An FDE is a software engineer who works on-site or embedded with a customer to get a product deployed and adopted. Think equal parts senior engineer, solutions architect, and trusted advisor.

Do your engineers represent our brand?

Yes, if you want. Most clients have our FDEs work under their company name with a company email, so the customer experiences one seamless team.

Which stacks do you cover?

TypeScript, Python, Go, and Java services; Postgres, Snowflake and BigQuery; AWS, GCP and Azure; plus the usual enterprise suspects — Salesforce, SAP, Okta, and ServiceNow.

Can you work on-site?

Yes. Engagements are remote by default, with on-site weeks for kickoffs, security-sensitive environments, or go-live — travel is billed at cost.

What if the deployment runs over?

If we miss the agreed definition of done for reasons within our control, we keep working at no extra cost until it's met.

Have a deployment that's stuck?

Tell us where it's blocked. An engineer — not a sales rep — replies within one business day with a first take.
