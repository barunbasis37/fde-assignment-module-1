Frequently asked

Everything else you need to know.

What exactly is a Forward Deployed Engineer? ▼

An FDE is a senior software engineer who works directly inside your customer's environment—on their Slack, in their codebase, at their desks—until the deployment is live and adopted. Think of them as a trusted engineer who speaks your product's language and your customer's technical constraints fluently.

Do your engineers represent our company? ▼

Yes, by design. Most customers prefer our FDEs to use your company email and represent your brand. They're embedded as part of your team, not a third-party vendor.

What tech stacks do you cover? ▼

TypeScript, Python, Go, Java, and Node.js on the application side. Postgres, Snowflake, BigQuery on data. AWS, GCP, Azure on infrastructure. We also have deep expertise in common SaaS integrations (Salesforce, SAP, Okta, ServiceNow) and legacy systems.

Can work be done fully remote? ▼

Absolutely. Most engagements are fully remote. For customer-sensitive environments or go-live week, we can be on-site—travel is billed at cost plus our standard rate.

What if a deployment runs over? ▼

If we miss our committed definition of done for reasons within our control, we keep working at no additional cost until it's met. We take accountability for outcomes, not just effort.
