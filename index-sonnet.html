FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out and we'll get back within one business day.

What exactly is a Forward Deployed Engineer?

A Forward Deployed Engineer is a senior software engineer who works directly inside your team or your customer's environment — writing code, debugging live systems, and shipping integrations — rather than working remotely from a backlog handed over by sales.

How fast can an engineer actually start?

Most engagements kick off within five business days of the discovery call, once scope and access are confirmed. Urgent production issues can be triaged same-week.

Do you work remote, on-site, or both?

Both. Many engagements are fully remote; for customer-facing rollouts or sensitive environments, our FDEs can work on-site for all or part of the engagement.

What happens to the code after the engagement ends?

Everything we build lives in your repositories under your license from day one. Every engagement closes with documentation and a hands-on knowledge-transfer session with your team.

How is this different from a staffing agency?

We only place senior engineers who've shipped production systems, we scope outcomes rather than headcount, and every engagement includes a named technical lead accountable for the result — not just the hours.

Ready to ship the integration that's been stuck?

Tell us what's blocked. We'll come back with a scoped plan and a start date within two business days.
